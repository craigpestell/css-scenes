About

Built with CSS.
Watched in a browser.

Each scene is one HTML file and one stylesheet, drawn and animated with the newest parts of CSS: sibling-index(), shape(), @property, offset-path and friends. A scene uses JavaScript or an image only when it truly needs one, and says so.

Open any scene and press How it’s made to read its write-up beside its code, or take it to CodePen. Controls changes its speed, colours and complexity, and Stats shows what it costs your browser.

Scenes respect reduced motion with a complete still frame, and fall back gracefully where a feature isn’t supported yet. No trackers, no web fonts, no third-party scripts.
